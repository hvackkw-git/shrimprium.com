I sketched out a breakout game played on another player's tank. You would break every platform block in their tank, then take home one block you liked.

I built a prototype and played it, but it wasn't fun. I scrapped it right away.

In the end, I felt there was only one thing I needed to do: focus on what matters most. I decided to concentrate only on how to connect better with the shrimp and how to make the tank feel alive.
