I'm working on a feature that lets you visit the animals' houses.

The plan is for you to get to know the animals and exchange gifts with them. The blocks and decorations the animals own won't be sold in the shop, so the only way to get them is through the animals themselves.

I hope this makes every tank truly its owner's own, decorated with things that came from the friendships you built rather than things you simply bought.

Do you have any ideas for this? If so, come and share them in the comments on [itch.io](https://itch.io/blog/1718037/lab-visiting-the-animals-houses).
