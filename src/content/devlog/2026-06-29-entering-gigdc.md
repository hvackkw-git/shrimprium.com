Hello, I'm Crafty Olive.

I've entered Shrimprium in the mobile game category of the general division of GIGDC (Global Indie Game Developers Competition), an indie game competition held in Korea.

From June 29 to July 13, the competition is collecting player feedback and running the first round of judging. Player feedback reportedly isn't reflected in the evaluation, but simply having you visit and follow along would be a big encouragement. (Unfortunately, it seems you need to sign up for an account to view the games.)

Whatever the result, I've made a world I love, and if there are people who come to love it with me, that would be a wonderful thing.
