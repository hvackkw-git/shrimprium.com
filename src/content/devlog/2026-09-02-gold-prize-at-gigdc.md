Hello, I'm Crafty Olive.

I have some happy personal news to share once again.

My game has won a gold prize at GIGDC (Global Indie Game Developers Competition), an indie game competition held in Korea.

I learned the unfamiliar craft of game development one step at a time, went through countless rounds of trial and error, and poured into the game my knowledge of heat, chemistry and energy modeling along with the things I love.

In the end, it has become a unique and rather strange game that resembles me a great deal. So I'm truly delighted that it seems to have been recognized as a game that brings people joy.

Looking at it myself, I think it scored highly on its ideas and design.

So, in return for this wonderful prize, I plan to put resources into the graphics from here on.
